# Sitemap

Project: Enriquez Project  
Website: https://purposefundgrp.com/  
Version: v1 (first-pass planning)

## 1) IA Goals
- Increase trust and conversion for business-owner applicants.
- Support a distinct ISO partner acquisition path.
- Create a foundation for functional tools in Resources.
- Keep navigation clear while preserving room for future expansion.

## 2) Primary Navigation
- Home
- Funding Programs (optional in phase 1)
- ISO Partnerships
- Resources
- Testimonials
- Company Principles
- Contact
- Apply Now (persistent CTA)

## 3) Audience Paths
### Path A: Business Owner (Get Funding)
- Entry points: Home, Funding Programs, Testimonials, Apply Now.
- Intent: evaluate credibility, understand offers, apply.
- CTA path: Learn Funding Options -> View Proof -> Apply Now.

### Path B: ISO Partner (Become a Partner)
- Entry points: Home split section, ISO Partnerships page, Contact.
- Intent: evaluate partnership economics and trust.
- CTA path: Review Partner Value -> Speak to Team -> Apply/Contact.

## 4) Page-Level Structure
## Home
Purpose: establish trust fast and route users into the correct funnel.

Recommended sections:
1. Hero: value proposition + primary Apply CTA + secondary ISO CTA.
2. Familiarity video block: Cade/Jason and brand story clips.
3. Credibility row: client logos, highlights, social proof snippets.
4. Funding outcomes: what problems are solved and for whom.
5. Testimonial preview: video and quote cards with links.
6. Tool preview: calculator/qualification teaser.
7. Process overview: how funding flow works.
8. Final CTA panel: Apply Now + speak with team.

## Funding Programs (Optional)
Purpose: explain product categories and reduce confusion before apply.

Recommended sections:
1. Program overview and positioning disclaimer.
2. Product cards (credit lines, revenue-based products, etc.).
3. Typical use cases and timeline expectations.
4. Qualification factors (high-level).
5. CTA to apply or speak with team.

## ISO Partnerships
Purpose: convert brokers/partners with a dedicated trust and economics narrative.

Recommended sections:
1. Partner-specific hero and value proposition.
2. Enriquez ISO visit videos (SkyCap, Irongate, Evergreen, Triumph, Suburban).
3. Value pillars: return, syndication, unique offers, no backdooring, upfront commissions.
4. Fundworks and retention messaging.
5. Partner process and support model.
6. CTA: partner apply and direct phone contact.

## Resources
Purpose: authority-building and lead nurturing.

Recommended sections:
1. Cost of Capital calculator.
2. MCA tracker request flow.
3. Podcast and thought leadership.
4. Free MCA stack offer.
5. Blog and market updates.

## Testimonials
Purpose: concentrated proof hub for decision-stage visitors.

Recommended sections:
1. Video testimonials.
2. Quote cards and mini case summaries.
3. Industry/category filters (future enhancement).
4. CTA to apply.

## Company Principles
Purpose: communicate philosophy and ethics to increase trust.

Recommended sections:
1. Mission and values.
2. How the team works with clients and partners.
3. Relationship principles and transparency commitments.
4. CTA to contact/apply.

## Contact
Purpose: clear communication paths for prospects and partners.

Recommended sections:
1. Contact methods (ISO rep, underwriter, email).
2. Social links (X, Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook).
3. Question form with routing intent.
4. Response-time expectation statement.

## Apply Now
Purpose: conversion endpoint with minimal friction.

Phase 1:
- Current external application path with improved context and expectations.

Phase 2+:
- Enhanced intake logic, data capture, and routing.

## 5) Utility Components Across Pages
- Sticky Apply CTA in top navigation.
- Trust badges/social proof strip.
- Footer with contact + social + compliance links.
- Optional floating "Talk to Team" CTA on mobile.

## 6) Future IA Expansion (Not Required in Phase 1)
- Eligibility Checker page.
- Partner onboarding flow.
- Resource detail templates (blog/podcast/tool pages).
- Dashboard or status-check portal (backend dependent).
