# Website Audit (First Pass)

Project: Enriquez Project  
Website reviewed: https://purposefundgrp.com/  
Date: 2026-03-07

## 1) Audit Objective
Evaluate the current website as a trust and lead-conversion system, then identify strategic upgrades across:
- Visual/UX structure.
- Functional tools.
- Backend/operational workflow.

## 2) Executive Summary
Current site appears to function primarily as a basic brochure with an external application link. The platform does not currently present a robust trust architecture, strong conversion sequencing, or structured lead operations. Highest-value opportunity is to rebuild the site as a dual-path funnel (business owner and ISO partner) with phased implementation.

## 3) Current-State Findings
## A. Positioning and Messaging
Findings:
- Value proposition is not sequenced for rapid trust formation.
- Limited differentiation and depth for decision-stage visitors.
- No obvious split narrative for business-owner vs ISO-partner intent.

Impact:
- Reduced confidence for high-intent traffic.
- More drop-off before application intent forms.

## B. Information Architecture
Findings:
- Site structure appears minimal and shallow.
- Key trust and conversion pages are underdeveloped or missing.
- Limited narrative flow from awareness -> proof -> action.

Impact:
- Users must infer too much before converting.
- Weak guidance through a funding decision journey.

## C. Trust and Social Proof
Findings:
- Testimonial/video strategy is not central to page hierarchy.
- Credibility assets are not organized into a proof progression.

Impact:
- Lost opportunity to reduce skepticism in a high-trust category.

## D. Conversion and CTA Strategy
Findings:
- Apply path seems linear and externally linked.
- CTA placement likely lacks strategic cadence across sections.
- Limited pre-application support tools.

Impact:
- Lower conversion efficiency.
- Fewer qualified applicants entering the intake flow.

## E. Intake and Operations
Findings:
- Current flow appears to rely on SignNow submission and email delivery.
- No visible structured system for lead tracking and status progression.

Impact:
- Risk of lead disorganization, delayed response, and missed follow-up.
- Limited visibility into funnel performance.

## 4) Opportunity Map
## Priority 1: Trust Architecture Rebuild (Phase 1)
- Video-forward familiarity sections.
- Testimonial and case proof hierarchy.
- Company principles and credibility reinforcement.

## Priority 2: Conversion Funnel Improvements (Phase 1/2)
- Better CTA placement and sequencing.
- Dedicated user paths for funding vs ISO partnership.
- Clear process explanation and expectation-setting.

## Priority 3: Functional Conversion Tools (Phase 2)
- Cost of Capital calculator.
- Qualification/eligibility estimator.
- Resource-driven nurture assets.

## Priority 4: Operational Backbone (Phase 3)
- Structured lead capture system.
- Workflow tracking and follow-up automation.
- Reporting on lead source, stage, and outcomes.

## 5) Risks if No Changes Are Made
- Continued lead leakage from manual inbox workflow.
- Lower trust and conversion relative to competitors.
- Inability to scale efficiently as inquiry volume grows.
- Weak measurement of marketing and conversion performance.

## 6) Recommended First Implementation Sequence
1. Confirm core positioning and audience split.
2. Launch phase 1 architecture and conversion-focused page structure.
3. Prepare calculator and eligibility specs with compliance language.
4. Define phase 3 data/workflow model before implementation.

## 7) Technical Notes for Planning
- Existing GoDaddy setup may constrain advanced feature development.
- Tool logic and workflow integrations should be designed as modular components.
- Future build stack should be selected after phase 1 requirements are locked.
