# Phase 1 Launch Week Checklist

Project: Enriquez Project  
Version: v1  
Date: 2026-03-07  
Purpose: Daily checklist for launch week execution and risk reduction.

## 1) Monday (T-5)
- Confirm all `P1` owners and due dates.
- Validate open dependencies and assign escalation owners.
- Confirm canonical apply URL decision owner and deadline.
- Review risk register and update ratings.

## 2) Tuesday (T-4)
- Confirm proof/media readiness status.
- Validate copy approval status for high-impact sections.
- Check contact details and routing requirements.
- Update action register and dashboard status.

## 3) Wednesday (T-3)
- Escalate unresolved critical dependencies.
- Confirm compliance review progress.
- Map completed build items to QA test IDs.
- Confirm launch communication owner availability.

## 4) Thursday (T-2)
- Execute pre-launch QA pass for available items.
- Confirm go/no-go gate status trend.
- Reconfirm rollback owner and trigger rules.
- Publish launch-risk snapshot.

## 5) Friday (T-1)
- Run launch-readiness checklist in full.
- Confirm all critical gates status.
- Lock launch-day timeline and participants.
- Send final pre-launch communication.

## 6) Launch Day (T-0)
- Execute launch runbook timeline.
- Run T+15 and T+45 smoke tests.
- Log incidents and responses.
- Publish stabilization update at T+120.

## 7) Day +1
- Capture first KPI snapshot.
- Record issues/lessons learned.
- Update completion report draft.
- Confirm Phase 2 transition brief kickoff date.
