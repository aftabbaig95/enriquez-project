# Phase 1 Onboarding Quickstart

Project: Enriquez Project  
Version: v1  
Date: 2026-03-07  
Purpose: Bring a new contributor to productive execution within 30-45 minutes.

## 1) What You Are Joining
- Phase 1 is focused on trust architecture and conversion clarity.
- Workstreams are separated into:
  - Visual/UX/Branding
  - Functional Features (planning-level in Phase 1)
  - Backend/Operations (planning-level in Phase 1)

## 2) First 15 Minutes
1. Read `docs/phase1-implementation-brief.md`.
2. Review `docs/phase1-master-index.md`.
3. Scan `docs/phase1-weekly-execution-dashboard.md`.

## 3) Next 15 Minutes
1. Open `docs/content-production-tracker-phase1.md` and locate `P1` rows.
2. Review owner allocations in `docs/owner-assignment-sheet-phase1.md`.
3. Check unresolved blockers in `docs/phase1-open-questions-register.md` and `docs/phase1-risk-register.md`.

## 4) Next 15 Minutes
1. Review execution controls:
- `docs/phase1-build-handoff-spec.md`
- `docs/phase1-qa-test-case-matrix.md`
- `docs/phase1-launch-readiness-checklist.md`
2. Confirm current sprint priorities from `docs/phase1-implementation-ticket-backlog.md`.

## 5) Immediate Actions for New Contributor
- Claim one `P1` row and confirm due date.
- Validate one blocked dependency and suggest mitigation.
- Update dashboard or weekly report with one verified status update.

## 6) Rules of Engagement
- Do not change CTA routing without updating build handoff and QA docs.
- Log scope changes in `phase1-change-request-log.md`.
- Log unresolved questions in `phase1-open-questions-register.md`.
- Keep master index updated when adding any new artifact.

## 7) Where to Ask and Escalate
- Content conflicts: Content Lead.
- Routing/tracking issues: Ops Lead.
- QA failures: QA Lead.
- Go/No-Go decisions: Launch Lead + Project Lead.

## 8) Success Definition for Your First Week
- Assigned items move from `Open/In Progress` toward `Ready/Approved`.
- At least one blocked dependency is unblocked or escalated with owner/date.
- Weekly dashboard and update reflect accurate status.
