# Phase 1 Glossary

Project: Enriquez Project  
Version: v1  
Date: 2026-03-07  
Purpose: Maintain shared definitions for terms used across planning and launch docs.

## Terms
- `Phase 1`: Trust and conversion structure delivery stage.
- `Phase 2`: Functional features stage (calculators, eligibility tools, richer flows).
- `Phase 3`: Backend and operational enhancement stage.
- `P1`: Launch-critical priority level.
- `P2`: High-value but non-blocking for immediate launch.
- `P3`: Optional or post-launch enhancement priority.
- `Canonical Apply URL`: Single approved destination used for primary apply CTAs.
- `CTA`: Call to Action (for example `Apply Now`, `Call Team`).
- `Proof Pack`: Minimum approved testimonials/logos/media required for trust sections.
- `Go/No-Go Gate`: Critical readiness check that determines launch eligibility.
- `Conditional Go`: Launch allowed with controlled known issues and mitigation owners.
- `Blocked`: Item that cannot proceed due to unresolved dependency.
- `Ready for Review`: Draft complete and waiting on approver decision.
- `Approval Evidence`: Record showing who approved what and when.
- `Handoff`: Transfer of responsibility between planning, build, QA, and launch teams.
- `Smoke Test`: Fast post-launch validation of critical user paths.
- `Routing Accuracy`: Correct classification and handling of Funding vs ISO inquiries.
- `Tracking Standard`: Agreed format for attribution parameters and source tagging.
- `Risk Register`: Central log of risks with probability/impact and mitigation owners.
- `Open Questions Register`: Central log for unresolved decisions with due dates.
- `Change Request`: Logged request that changes scope, timeline, quality, risk, or cost.
- `Execution Dashboard`: Weekly snapshot of status, blockers, and next actions.
- `Completion Report`: End-of-phase summary documenting outcomes and carry-forward items.
- `Transition Brief`: Handoff document from one phase to the next.

## Usage Notes
- If a new recurring term appears in meetings or docs, add it here.
- Keep definitions concise and execution-focused.
