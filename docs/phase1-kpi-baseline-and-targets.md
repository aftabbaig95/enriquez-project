- Conversion KPIs
- Engagement KPIs
- Operational KPIs

## 6) Instrumentation Checklist
- Canonical apply URL confirmed and tagged.
- CTA tracking standards documented and implemented.
- Contact form captures intent category.
- Key section interaction events configured (FAQ, testimonials, process, final CTA).
- Reporting owner assigned for weekly KPI updates.

## 7) KPI Review Ritual
- Monday: refresh prior-week KPI values.
- Wednesday: identify anomalies and investigate drivers.
- Friday: summarize KPI movement in weekly stakeholder update.

## 8) KPI Decision Thresholds
- Green: >=75% of KPI targets on trajectory.
- Yellow: 50%-74% on trajectory; mitigation required.
- Red: <50% on trajectory; action plan required within 5 business days.
