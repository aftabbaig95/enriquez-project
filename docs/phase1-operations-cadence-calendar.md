# Phase 1 Operations Cadence Calendar

Project: Enriquez Project  
Version: v1  
Date: 2026-03-07  
Purpose: Define weekly operating rhythm for execution, review, and launch readiness.

## 1) Weekly Cadence Overview
- Monday: assignment and status alignment.
- Tuesday: build and content production focus.
- Wednesday: blocker resolution and decision escalation.
- Thursday: QA preparation and risk review.
- Friday: executive reporting and next-week planning.

## 2) Day-by-Day Operating Plan

### Monday: Alignment Day
- Update `content-production-tracker-phase1.md` statuses.
- Confirm `P1` owners and due dates in `owner-assignment-sheet-phase1.md`.
- Review `open-questions-register` for overdue decisions.

### Tuesday: Build + Content Execution
- Progress implementation tickets from backlog.
- Validate incoming assets against asset packet specs.
- Run copy review for high-impact sections.

### Wednesday: Decision and Dependency Day
- Resolve or escalate blocked dependencies.
- Update decision log with finalized answers.
- Re-score risks and assign mitigation owners.

### Thursday: QA Readiness Day
- Map completed work to QA test IDs.
- Pre-validate CTA routes and contact paths.
- Confirm launch readiness checklist progress.

### Friday: Reporting and Planning Day
- Publish weekly execution dashboard.
- Publish KPI weekly report and stakeholder update.
- Confirm next-week top 3 priorities and owners.

## 3) Weekly Meeting Set (Suggested)

| Meeting | Day | Duration | Owner | Output |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Weekly Kickoff | Monday | 30 min | Project Lead | Updated priorities and owner confirmations |
| Blocker Triage | Wednesday | 30 min | Launch Lead | Escalation decisions and due dates |
| QA/Launch Prep | Thursday | 30 min | QA Lead | Readiness status and defect plan |
| Stakeholder Review | Friday | 45 min | Project Lead | Weekly update + KPI movement + next actions |

## 4) Recurring Deliverables by Friday
- `phase1-weekly-execution-dashboard.md` (filled)
- `phase1-kpi-weekly-report-template.md` (filled copy)
- `weekly-stakeholder-update-template.md` (filled copy)
- `phase1-risk-register.md` updated
- `phase1-open-questions-register.md` updated

## 5) Escalation Timing Rules
- Any critical blocker unresolved by Wednesday EOD escalates same day.
- Any compliance open question unresolved by Thursday EOD escalates to decision owners.
- Any `P1` QA failure unresolved by launch-minus 48h triggers go/no-go review.
