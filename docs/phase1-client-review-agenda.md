# Phase 1 Client Review Agenda

Project: Enriquez Project  
Version: v1  
Date: 2026-03-07  
Purpose: Provide a structured agenda for client review sessions during Phase 1.

## 1) Meeting Objective
- Confirm alignment on structure, messaging, proof assets, and launch readiness.
- Close open decisions that block `P1` execution.

## 2) Duration and Format
- Recommended duration: 45-60 minutes.
- Format:
  - 10 min: status and progress recap
  - 20 min: walkthrough of wireframe/build priorities
  - 15 min: decisions and owner assignments
  - 10 min: risks, deadlines, and next actions

## 3) Pre-Read Pack (Send 24h Before)
- `docs/phase1-master-index.md`
- `docs/wireframe-phase1.md`
- `docs/wireframe-copy-placeholders-phase1.md`
- `docs/content-production-tracker-phase1.md`
- `docs/phase1-launch-readiness-checklist.md`

## 4) Agenda Script

### A. Progress Snapshot
- What was completed this week.
- What is currently in progress.
- What is blocked and why.

### B. Page-by-Page Review (P1)
- Home: hero, proof, CTA flow.
- ISO: partner messaging and media.
- Testimonials: proof hierarchy.
- Contact: intent routing and contact trust.
- Apply Entry: conversion handoff clarity.

### C. Decision Items (Must Resolve)
- Final testimonial assets and approvals.
- Final partner/team videos and captions.
- Canonical apply URL and tracking standard.
- Compliance signoff path for claims language.

### D. Assignment and Due Dates
- Confirm owner and due date for each unresolved `P1` row.
- Confirm escalation owner for blocked dependencies.

### E. Launch Readiness Snapshot
- Review go/no-go gates.
- Confirm top risks and mitigations.
- Confirm next review milestone.
