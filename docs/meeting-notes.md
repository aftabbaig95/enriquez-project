# Meeting Notes

Project: Enriquez Project  
Date: 2026-03-07  
Prepared by: Project Team

## 1) Project Intent
The client wants a full website revamp for Purpose Fund Group that improves trust, increases qualified leads, and supports future operational growth. This is not a visual refresh only.

Current website: https://purposefundgrp.com/

## 2) Business Objectives (Confirmed)
- Build familiarity and trust through video-led storytelling and testimonials.
- Increase lead generation and conversion through stronger calls to action.
- Add functional tools over time (for example, finance calculators).
- Represent brand identity, social proof, and company principles clearly.
- Support two audience journeys:
  - Business owners seeking funding.
  - ISO partners/brokers evaluating partnership.

## 3) Current-State Observations
- Site appears to be built in GoDaddy Website Builder.
- Current structure is minimal and brochure-like.
- Trust-building sequence is limited (insufficient proof flow).
- Application path appears to be SignNow form -> email inbox -> manual follow-up.
- No evident structured lead system, applicant tracking, or CRM workflow.

## 4) Strategic Framing for Scope
Work should be planned and priced in three distinct lanes:
- Visual and UX improvements.
- Functional tools and custom logic features.
- Backend and operational enhancements.

Reason: page styling, custom calculators, and lead workflow systems are different workstreams with different effort and risk profiles.

## 7) Conversion Strategy Direction
Recommended homepage funnel:
1. Immediate credibility and trust proof.
2. Clear value proposition for funding outcomes.
3. Testimonial and case evidence.
4. Functional pre-qualification or calculator touchpoint.
5. Apply CTA.
6. Secondary path for ISO partners.

## 8) Immediate Deliverable Strategy
Initial consultation output should be diagnostic and strategic:
- Audit findings and risk map.
- High-level sitemap and conversion structure.
- Scope phasing with clear lane boundaries.

Avoid giving full production-level design systems, complete copy decks, or production tool builds before scope alignment.

## 9) Open Questions for Next Client Session
